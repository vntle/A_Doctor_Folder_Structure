# 01_Documents — Nhat-Le Vo

> *Structure adapted from the PhD Folder Structure template by Demerdash, Wilbrandt & Dockhorn.
> Original: [github.com/RDMJeanne/FolderStructure](https://github.com/RDMJeanne/FolderStructure)*

This folder holds all documents that are not research output — administrative paperwork,
reading notes, literature, courses, and funding materials. Nothing in here is raw data or code.

---

## My Structure

```
01_Documents/
├── 01_Personal/              CV, certificates, personal records
├── 02_Administrative/
│   ├── MARUM/                Contract, office access, HR — MARUM-specific
│   ├── ExcellenceCluster/    Cluster obligations, annual reports, cluster meetings
│   ├── UniBremen/            Enrollment, library access, IT, student services
│   └── DMP/                  Data Management Plan — see M_DMP_Geoscience.md
├── 03_Notes/
│   ├── Supervision_Meetings/ Named: YYYY-MM-DD_Supervision_[topic].md
│   ├── Reading_Notes/        Notes on papers and books I have read
│   └── Seminar_Notes/        MARUM colloquia, cluster workshops, summer schools
├── 04_Literature/
│   ├── 00_PDFs/              Raw downloaded PDFs (managed also via Zotero)
│   ├── 01_Annotated/         Annotated versions — exported from Zotero/Hypothesis
│   └── 02_Reviews/           My written literature review drafts
├── 05_Courses/               PhD programme courses, certificates, workshop materials
└── 06_Funding_Travel/        Grant applications, travel requests, expense reports
```

---

## Notes on Usage

- **Supervision meetings** are the most important notes to keep current.
  I date-stamp every file: `2026-05-28_Supervision_Jotul_update.md`.
- **Literature** is managed primarily in Zotero; this folder stores PDF exports and
  written reviews, not the Zotero library itself.
- **DMP** is a living document — I revisit it every 6 months with my supervisor.
  See `02_Administrative/DMP/M_DMP_Geoscience.md` for my current plan.
